# SenRate

**Pass rate & consistency tracker** by **Sen**.

Know exactly how consistent you are on a level and what to practice next.

## Features
- **Every run you do** (25-60, 27-100...) in a **Runs** tab and on the **level complete screen**, with noclip runs shown apart.
- **100%s from every start pos**, pass rate, streaks and an estimated chance of a full run.
- **Smart analysis per part**: SenRate separates how you do a part when you *practice* it (start pos close to it) from how you do it *deep into long runs*. If you already beat 87-100 again and again, 95-100 won't be called your worst part - and if you only die there in full runs, it's flagged as **nerves (choke)**.
- **Tips**: your worst part, what you already master, chokes, trend and a practice plan.
- **Death histogram** per percent, like a death tracker.
- **All-time / Session / Recent** views.
- **Noclip**: every hit ends a run and starts a new one until the end of the level (25-27, 27-60, 60-100), or only up to your first hit, or ignored.
- **Gray X** deletes this session's runs, **red X** deletes the whole history of the level.
- Button in the **pause menu**, the **level page** and the **level complete screen**.
- English & Español.
